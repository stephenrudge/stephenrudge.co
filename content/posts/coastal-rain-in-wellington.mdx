Wellington doesn't apologize for the wind. It leans into it — jackets zipped, coffee cups held with both hands, conversations shouted companionably across street corners.

## A day with rain in the forecast

I walked the waterfront anyway. Seagulls contested chips. The harbor flexed under a low sky. Museums offered shelter when the rain thickened, then spit me back out into salt air.

## Practical notes

- Pack a real rain shell, not a fashion one.
- Cable car up for views; walk down for neighborhoods.
- Evenings are for bookshops and bowls of something warm.

The best travel days aren't always clear. Sometimes the story is the weather itself — and how a city moves through it without flinching.
